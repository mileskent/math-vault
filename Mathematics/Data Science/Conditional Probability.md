The [[Probability]] of $A$, $P(A)$, given $B$ has occurred ($P(B) > 0$)
$$
P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}
$$

# Simple Sample Space
If the [[Sample Space]] is a [[Sample Space#Simple Sample Space]], i.e. where $\forall \omega \in S,\ P(\{\omega\}) = \frac{1}{|S|}$, and all of the above is already true, then the following is valid.

$$
P(A \mid B) = 
\frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}
=
\frac{\frac{|A \cap B|}{|S|}}{\frac{|B|}{|S|}} = 
\frac{|A \cap B|}{|B|}
$$
