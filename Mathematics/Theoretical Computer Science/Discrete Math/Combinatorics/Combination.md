A selection of elements from a [[Mathematics/Set Theory/Set|Set]] where order *doesn't* matter. "n choose k". 
$$
C(n, k) = C_{n,k} = 
\begin{pmatrix}
n\\
k
\end{pmatrix}=
\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!} = \frac{P(n,k)}{P(k,k)}
$$

It is also called "Binomial Coefficient", as it forms the terms in *Pascal's Triangle*, which are coefficients of $n^\text{th}$ degree binomial when expanded into a [[Polynomial]].
